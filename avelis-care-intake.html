Why This Form Exists

Care that fits requires
understanding that goes
beyond the diagnosis.

Every family navigating dementia has a different story. The person in your care has a history, a personality, preferences, and a particular way of experiencing the world — and none of that appears in a medical record.

This form is how we learn who they are. It shapes who we match with your family, how we prepare ythe caregiver we introduce, and how we approach the first visit. The more you share, the more precisely we can care.

Take your time. There are no wrong answers — only honest ones.

What the Form Covers
  • 01
    Your Family Contact details, your relationship to the person in care, and who else is involved in decisions.
  • 02
    The Person in Care Their name, diagnosis, stage, medical history, and current medications.
  • 03
    Daily Life & Routine Who they are as a person — their history, interests, comforts, and what a good day looks like.
  • 04
    Care Needs Mobility, daily assistance required, behavioral patterns, and what has worked — or hasn't.
  • 05
    Caregiver Wellbeing How you are doing — honestly. Your experience of caregiving matters as much as everything else.
  • 06
    Getting Started Which services, urgency, schedule, and anything else we should know before we speak.
Strictly Confidential
Your responses are held privately within Avelis Care and will never be shared with third parties under any circumstances.
