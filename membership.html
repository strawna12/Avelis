Common Questions

What families ask about
membership.

Is there a minimum commitment period?
No. Plans are billed month-to-month with no long-term contract. We believe families should stay because the support is valuable — and most do, because it is. You can pause, adjust, or cancel at any time with appropriate notice.
How is my plan customized?
Every membership begins with an onboarding assessment — a dedicated conversation where we learn your loved one's health situation, goals, and family structure. From there, we build a specific support plan within your chosen tier. The tier sets the framework; the plan makes it personal.
Does insurance cover Avelis membership?
Avelis services are typically private-pay and are not covered by Medicare or standard health insurance, as they fall outside the definition of skilled home health care. Some long-term care insurance policies may cover non-medical care coordination or support services. We're happy to help you review your policy during your consultation.
Can I upgrade or change my plan over time?
Yes — and this is common. Many families start with the Essentials plan and move to Comprehensive as needs grow. We conduct quarterly plan reviews and proactively recommend adjustments when your situation changes.
What's the difference between the Comprehensive and Private (Concierge) plans?
The primary differences are frequency and access. The Private plan includes weekly in-home visits, direct coordinator phone access, appointment accompaniment, and priority crisis response support — designed for clients who need more intensive, more frequent engagement. Comprehensive is best for families who need consistent coordination and advocacy without the full weekly in-person presence.
