Our Philosophy

Built around the
person, not the diagnosis.

Avelis Care is not a staffing service. We are a curated practice — selecting caregivers for temperament as much as skill, and matching them with intention rather than availability.

Every person living with dementia deserves to feel known and at ease in their own home. Every family deserves someone they can trust without reservation. We exist to make both possible.

The Avelis Standard
Consistency over convenience. Presence over procedure. Showing up — reliably, calmly, without fail.
Intentional Matching
We study your loved one's history, rhythms, and personality before a single introduction is made. Compatibility is not incidental — it is the work.
Dementia-Informed Presence
Our caregivers are trained in communication, not just tasks — meeting people where they are, without correction, urgency, or distress.
Protected Consistency
The same face, the same calm, every time. For someone living with dementia, this is not a preference — it is a clinical and emotional necessity.
Dignity Without Exception
Not a corporate principle — a daily practice. Every caregiver we place understands this, and is held to it.

Why Avelis

For families who
refuse to settle.

Home care options are plentiful. What is rare is care that is truly attentive — that sees your family as people, not a placement.

Avelis exists for families who know the difference.

i.
Matching by Character, Not Calendar

We do not assign based on availability. We match based on who is right — and we take the time that requires, because getting it wrong costs far more.

ii.
Dementia Is Not One Thing

It shifts. It surprises. It asks for patience that cannot be taught in an afternoon. Every Avelis caregiver has been vetted for understanding — not just compliance.

iii.
Consistency as Standard, Not Bonus

Familiarity is therapeutic. We protect the same-caregiver relationship with discipline — because for someone living with dementia, disruption is not inconvenient. It is a setback.

iv.
You Reach a Person

No call centers. No intake queues. When you contact Avelis, someone answers — and that someone knows your family's name.
