Diagnosis & Medical History.

Clinical diagnosis, stage, secondary conditions, medications, and relevant medical context.

Section 03 of 09

Who They Are.

The most important section. A caregiver who knows this person — their history, personality, and joys — provides meaningfully better care.

Complete this section thoroughly. These details directly shape caregiver matching and inform how ythe caregiver we introduce approaches every interaction. The emotional quality of care depends on it.
Section 04 of 09

Daily Routine.

Schedule, routines, and dietary needs. Consistency in routine is therapeutic — the more specific, the better.

Section 05 of 09

Mobility & Care Needs.

Functional status, ADL assistance required, safety concerns, and equipment.

Section 06 of 09

Behavioral & Cognitive Profile.

Behavioral symptoms, triggers, de-escalation approaches, and communication capacity. This section directly informs how the caregiver will handle difficult moments.

Section 07 of 09

Emergency Protocols.

Emergency contacts, call order, and special instructions. In any emergency: 911 first, then family, then Avelis.

Standard protocol: In any medical emergency, the caregiver calls 911 first. Then the primary family contact. Then Avelis. Caregivers do not perform medical intervention. Note any exceptions or family preferences below.
