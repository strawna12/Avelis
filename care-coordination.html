Common Questions

What families ask us
about care coordination.

Does Avelis replace my loved one's medical team?
No — and that's an important distinction. We coordinate around the medical team, not instead of them. Our role is to help organize, communicate, and ensure that clinical recommendations are followed through. We complement your loved one's providers; we don't replace them.
My parent lives locally but I'm out of state. Can Avelis keep me informed?
Absolutely — this is one of the most common situations we support. We provide regular written updates, are available by phone and message, and serve as your trusted local presence so distance doesn't mean being left out of your parent's care.
What does a care coordination engagement actually look like day to day?
It depends on your loved one's situation. For some families, it means a weekly check-in, tracking two upcoming specialist appointments, and a bi-weekly family update email. For others, it means full active coordination across five providers during a complex medical period. We scale to what's needed.
Is care coordination available as a standalone service or only through membership?
Both. Care coordination is included in our Comprehensive and Concierge membership plans, and is also available as a standalone engagement for families navigating a specific period — a hospitalization, a new diagnosis, or a care transition.
