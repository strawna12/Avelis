Who We're Looking For

Character first.
Always.

Skills and certification matter. But temperament — composure, patience, the ability to be genuinely present — matters most. We are selective because our families are trusting us with something irreplaceable.

  • Experience supporting individuals with dementia or Alzheimer's disease
  • Calm under pressure — your steadiness regulates the people in your care
  • Maturity to navigate sensitive family dynamics with grace
  • Commitment to consistency — same face, same warmth, every visit
  • HHA or CNA preferred; substantial experience considered without certification
  • Reliable transportation within the greater St. Petersburg area
What You'll Be Doing

The role,
clearly stated.

As a non-medical private caregiver with Avelis, your responsibilities include:

  • Companion presence and genuine engagement throughout the visit
  • Meal preparation and mealtime assistance per dietary needs
  • Personal care — bathing, dressing, grooming, and hygiene
  • Toileting and incontinence care, handled with dignity
  • Medication reminders (not administration)
  • Mobility assistance and fall prevention
  • Light housekeeping and laundry
  • Safety supervision and wandering prevention
  • Sundowning and behavioral de-escalation
  • Reminiscence, conversation, music, and activity engagement
  • Overnight supervision when assigned to overnight shifts
  • Post-visit reporting to Avelis after every shift

No medical procedures. No clinical tasks. Full presence — as independent caregivers matched to your family.
