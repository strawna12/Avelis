Who We're Looking For

Character first.
Always.

The quality of care someone receives in their home is shaped almost entirely by the person providing it. Skills matter. Certification matters. But temperament — composure, patience, the willingness to be truly present — matters most.

We are selective because our families are trusting us with something irreplaceable.

  • Experience supporting individuals living with dementia or Alzheimer's disease
  • The ability to remain calm in difficult moments — your steadiness is contagious
  • Genuine communication skills and the maturity to navigate sensitive family dynamics
  • A real commitment to consistency — the same face, the same warmth, every time
  • HHA or CNA preferred; candidates with substantial experience will be considered
  • Reliable transportation within the greater St. Petersburg area
What Makes Avelis Different

You are not
a roster entry.
You are known.

We are a small, deliberate team. Every person on it is matched thoughtfully, supported directly, and treated with the same standard we ask them to hold.

Placement With Purpose
You are placed with clients whose needs and personality align with how you work. Not scattered across unfamiliar situations — introduced and established with care.
Consistency Protected
Wherever possible, you return to the same client. This protects the people in your care — and protects your sense of purpose in the work.
You Are Not Left to Figure It Out
When a client's needs shift or a family dynamic changes, you are not alone in it. We remain reachable, communicative, and genuinely invested in how things are going.
Pay That Reflects the Work
Dementia care is specialized. If you have the skill — truly have it — you are compensated accordingly and placed where it makes a real difference.

Five Questions

No trick questions. No expected answers. An opportunity to show us how you think — and why you do this work.

01
Question One

02
Question Two

03

04
Question Four
