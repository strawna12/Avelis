Why Avelis

A different kind of
healthcare role.

If you've worked in healthcare, you've probably felt the frustration of knowing what a patient or family needs — and watching the system fail to provide it. The follow-up that doesn't happen. The discharge instructions nobody reads. The family that doesn't know who to call.

Avelis was built precisely to address that gap. Working here means working in a role where you have the time, the structure, and the mandate to actually do right by families — not just process them.

We're a small, values-driven team building something we believe in. If that's the kind of environment where you do your best work, keep reading.

What We Look For

Character first.
Credentials second.

The right person for Avelis leads with genuine care and follows with competence. Here's what we look for in everyone who joins our team.

Genuine Compassion
Not performed warmth — real investment in the people you serve. Families can tell the difference immediately, and so can we during the interview process.
Clinical or Healthcare Background
We work best with people who understand how the healthcare system works — RNs, LPNs, social workers, care managers, medical assistants, and others with real clinical or coordination experience.
Exceptional Communication
With families, with providers, and with our team. Written and verbal. Clear, warm, and professional — especially when the situation is difficult or emotional.
Organizational Discipline
We coordinate complex situations across multiple stakeholders. Nothing can fall through the cracks. We need people who are naturally organized and proactively thorough.
Discretion and Integrity
We work with families during vulnerable moments. Confidentiality and professional boundaries are non-negotiable — and we expect them to come naturally, not be enforced.
Initiative
We're a growing company. The right people here see what needs doing and do it — without waiting to be told. If you thrive in a structured-but-dynamic environment, you'll fit here well.
