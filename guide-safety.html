Home Safety Checklist

A room-by-room
safety audit.

Falls are the leading cause of injury in adults over 65 — and most happen at home. This checklist walks you through every room to identify risks and simple fixes. Work through it with a family member, and revisit it every six months as needs change.

Priority:

Medium — Address within 1 month
Low — Address when possible
