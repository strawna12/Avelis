The Service

Caring for someone with dementia is extraordinary work. You shouldn't have to do it without relief.

Private respite care exists so that you can step away — to rest, to attend to your own life, to simply exhale — without leaving your loved one in the care of a stranger. caregiver in the Avelis networks arrive prepared, calm, and already familiar with what matters to the person in your home.

This is not coverage. It is a considered handoff — from one trusted presence to another.

What This Looks Like

Respite care through Avelis is arranged around your schedule, not ours. Whether you need a few hours on a Tuesday morning or a recurring block of time each week, we structure support around what actually works for your family.

Before care begins, ythe caregiver we introduce is briefed on your loved one's preferences, routine, sensitivities, and what a good day looks like for them. We do not send someone in cold.

And wherever possible, the same caregiver returns each time — because familiarity is not a luxury in dementia care. It is the standard.

Who This Serves

You don't have to
be at the limit
to ask for help.

Respite care is for any family caregiver carrying consistent responsibility for someone with dementia — whether or not you feel you've earned a break. You have.

  • Spouses and partners who are the primary caregiver at home
  • Adult children managing care alongside their own families and careers
  • Families transitioning from facility care back to home care
  • Caregivers who need consistent weekly time to restore themselves
  • Families navigating a change in condition who need additional support
  • Anyone who simply needs a few reliable hours of calm, covered absence
